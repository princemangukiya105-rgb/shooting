We recommend reaching out 8 to 14 months before your wedding date. Because we cap our calendar at 25 commissions per year to ensure bespoke creative attention, prime dates in autumn, winter, and spring book up quickly. A 30% retainer and signed agreement reserves your date exclusively.

We are seasoned international travelers. Our crew holds multi-entry visas for Europe (Schengen), the UK, Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and the USA. We coordinate all our flights, local transit, and equipment carnets. For destination weddings, we arrive at least 24 to 48 hours prior to your celebrations to scout locations and light trajectories.

We hold ourselves to a strict delivery schedule:

  • Sneak Peek Gallery: 40 to 60 fully edited high-res images delivered in 72 hours so you can share your announcement immediately.
  • Full Photo Collection: 600 to 1,200+ retouched images in a private cloud gallery within 4 to 6 weeks.
  • Cinema Teaser (60 seconds) & Master Film (15-25 mins): Delivered in 4K HDR within 8 to 12 weeks.

We film with full-frame cinema cameras including the Sony FX6, Sony FX3, and ARRI Alexa Mini systems paired with fast prime lenses. For photography, we capture with high-resolution Sony Alpha and Leica systems alongside real 35mm film rolls. Every ceremony is recorded with dual 32-bit float wireless microphones to prevent audio distortion.

Yes, drone aerial cinematography is included in all our multi-day destination collections. Our pilots are licensed and prioritize safety, local airspace regulations, and weather feasibility.

Yes, absolutely. When one cohesive team handles both photos and cinema, our artists move together in harmony. Photographers don't walk into the filmmaker's frame, and filmmakers don't crowd the couple during portraits. The visual aesthetic, color grading, and tone also remain beautifully unified.

Raw video footage (every unedited 4K clip captured across all ceremony rituals, speeches, and dances) is provided on an encrypted high-speed SSD drive in our Couture packages or as an add-on. For photography, we deliver fully retouched high-resolution JPEGs.

We invite your input on musical genres, meaningful songs, and favorite artists during our pre-wedding questionnaire. Our editors select and license tracks that harmonize with the visual rhythm and emotional arc of your film.

We follow a strict 3-2-1 backup protocol:

  • All our cameras write to dual memory cards simultaneously.
  • Every night of your wedding, all footage is backed up onto two separate rugged SSD drives.
  • Upon returning to our studio, files are uploaded to our secure RAID server and offsite encrypted cloud storage.

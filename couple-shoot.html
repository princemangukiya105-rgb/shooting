The Editorial Guide

How We Create Magic Together

Everything you need to know about preparing for your couple shoot.

01

Wardrobe & Palette

We recommend earthy, muted neutrals: ivory, sage, champagne, terracotta, and soft linen fabrics. Flowing dresses catch the breeze, while textures add depth to cinema color grading.

02

Chasing The Golden Hour

We shoot during the first 90 minutes after dawn and the final 90 minutes before dusk. The low sun creates warm rim lighting, gentle flares, and soft shadows on the skin.

03

Building Camera Trust

A pre-wedding shoot allows you to discover our non-intrusive shooting style. By the time your wedding day arrives, being in front of our lenses feels completely natural.
